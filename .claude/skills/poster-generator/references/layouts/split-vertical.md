# Split Vertical

## Composition Structure

- Divide poster into left and right vertical zones.
- Use one zone for imagery and the other for headline/details.
- Let one side dominate slightly to avoid dead symmetry.
- Use a strong vertical edge, soft gradient boundary, or overlapping object to connect zones.

## Hierarchy Rules

1. Dominant side sets mood or message.
2. Opposite side delivers headline and action.
3. Footer/corner stores metadata.

## Text Placement

- Align headline and details to one consistent vertical grid.
- Avoid splitting a phrase across both panels unless intentional.

## Image Treatment

Use cropped product/person/photo on one side; keep the other side calmer.

## Spacing Rules

Maintain a central gutter. Use margins large enough that the split feels designed, not accidental.

## Aspect-Ratio Behavior

Works in `4:5`, `2:3`, `3:4`, `16:9`, and `1:1`. In narrow `9:16`, use a 40/60 split rather than 50/50.

## Avoid

Equal-weight halves, two busy panels, weak contrast, and duplicated focal points.
