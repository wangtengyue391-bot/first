# Full-Bleed Image Overlay

## Composition Structure

- Use one full-bleed image as the poster field.
- Place headline and details over controlled low-noise zones.
- Add gradient, translucent panel, blur, or shadow only where needed for readability.
- Keep composition cinematic and immersive.

## Hierarchy Rules

1. Image mood and subject create immediate impact.
2. Headline anchors interpretation.
3. Source-supplied details sit in a stable overlay or footer; action copy must remain plain typography, never a UI button.

## Text Placement

- Place headline over negative space or dark/light overlay.
- Keep small text in a panel, footer, or margin.
- Avoid text across faces, products, or complex textures.

## Image Treatment

Use strong photography, cinematic lighting, depth, atmosphere, and clean subject-background separation.

## Spacing Rules

Protect edges with safe margins. Use overlay panels sparingly.

## Aspect-Ratio Behavior

Strong across all ratios; choose image crop around subject safety and text zones.

## Avoid

Busy background text placement, over-filtered photos, generic stock imagery, and low-contrast typography.
