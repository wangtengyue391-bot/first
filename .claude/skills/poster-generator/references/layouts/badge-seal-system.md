# Badge / Seal System

## Composition Structure

- Use a central badge, seal, medallion, or emblem as the organizing device.
- Arrange supporting text radially, stacked, or in bands around it.
- Add small motifs only when they support the badge identity.
- Keep outer poster field calmer than the central mark.

## Hierarchy Rules

1. Badge shape attracts first attention.
2. Main title within/near badge confirms topic.
3. Supporting details surround or sit below.

## Text Placement

- Use short curved or stacked text only if legibility remains high.
- Keep required details horizontal when possible.
- Use consistent spacing around the badge.

## Image Treatment

Use emblematic illustration, product mark, hand-drawn icon, stamp texture, or simple symbolic object.

## Spacing Rules

Let the badge breathe. Avoid filling every ring with text.

## Aspect-Ratio Behavior

Strong in `1:1`, `2:3`, `3:4`, and `4:5`. In `9:16`, stack badge above details.

## Avoid

Overly complex seals, illegible curved microtext, fake logos, and cluttered decorative rings.
