# Collage / Montage

## Composition Structure

- Layer cutouts, photo fragments, torn paper, stickers, marks, or graphic pieces.
- Use one dominant anchor element to prevent chaos.
- Integrate headline as a deliberate layer, not an afterthought.
- Balance dense collage areas with quiet negative space.

## Hierarchy Rules

1. Anchor image or headline provides first read.
2. Collage fragments add context and mood.
3. Details sit in a clean label, footer, or card.

## Text Placement

- Use headline as a strong typographic layer.
- Keep small required text on calm patches or labels.
- Avoid placing important text across many textures.

## Image Treatment

Use editorial cutouts, paper edges, scanning texture, halftone, tape, staples, or controlled mixed media.

## Spacing Rules

Layering may be dense, but reading zones must remain clear.

## Aspect-Ratio Behavior

Works in `2:3`, `4:5`, `1:1`, and `9:16`. In `16:9`, use panoramic layering.

## Avoid

Random scrapbook clutter, too many unrelated images, illegible text, and inconsistent lighting between fragments.
