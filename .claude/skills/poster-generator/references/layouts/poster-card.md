# Poster Card

## Composition Structure

- Place a card-like content block over a background field.
- Use the card for headline, key visual, or details.
- Let the background provide mood through color, gradient, photo, or texture.
- Add depth subtly through shadow, blur, border, or layering.

## Hierarchy Rules

1. Card acts as the main reading container.
2. Background supports mood without stealing focus.
3. Source-supplied details sit inside or directly attached to the card; action copy must remain plain typography without a control-like container.

## Text Placement

- Keep headline and details within card safe padding.
- Use a clean typographic stack.
- Avoid placing required text in noisy background areas.

## Image Treatment

Use product/object cutouts, abstract 3D, portrait crops, or small editorial images inside the card.

## Spacing Rules

Card padding and outer margins must be generous. Avoid edge-to-edge cramped cards.

## Aspect-Ratio Behavior

Works especially well in `4:5`, `1:1`, `9:16`, and `2:3`.

## Avoid

Generic app-card UI, fake interface controls, excessive shadows, and tiny card text.
