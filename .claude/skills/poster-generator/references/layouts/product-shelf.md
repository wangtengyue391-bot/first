# Product Shelf

## Composition Structure

- Present product as a hero object, shelf row, grid, or curated lineup.
- Use source-supplied benefit callouts and price/offer blocks around the product hierarchy. Do not invent action copy.
- Keep background controlled so product remains desirable.
- Use consistent baseline, shadow, and scale logic for multiple products.

## Hierarchy Rules

1. Product or product collection is first read.
2. Offer/headline explains value.
3. Source-supplied details complete the product story.

## Text Placement

- Place price/offer in a strong badge or clean label.
- Keep product name close to the product.
- Avoid covering important product features.

## Image Treatment

Use crisp product rendering, premium lighting, clean shadows, reflections, or ingredient/context accents.

## Spacing Rules

Products need breathing room. Callouts should not touch product edges.

## Aspect-Ratio Behavior

Works in `4:5`, `1:1`, `2:3`, and `16:9`. Use vertical stacking for `9:16`.

## Avoid

Distorted packaging, inaccurate labels, cluttered callouts, fake brand marks, and messy product scale.
